The problem, and what we’ve heard so far

Our team started from one problem statement: small business owners are overwhelmed balancing admin tasks with the larger work of running the company. That strain can lead to lost profit and delayed meetings.

Then came our first customer discovery interviews, with a small business owner and a CEO. Both described having to double-check work or answer follow-up questions.

From our problem statement, Oct 15, 2026

“Main issue is trust with AI and virtual assistants.”

Interviews so far: 2. The next round puts this prototype in front of owners.

So Right Hand is designed around the owner’s review: each finished task comes back with its work shown.

A second example: overdue invoice reminders

Our hypothesis: owners will hand off routine admin if checking the finished work takes less time than doing it themselves.

So far we have talked with owners about the problem. Next we put this prototype in front of them and ask:

  • Would this reduce your checking time?
  • What would you still need to verify?
  • Which task would you trust it with first?

Questions we expect

What if it gets something wrong?
You see the sources and the math before anything is final, and anything it isn’t sure about comes to you as a question. Nothing goes out until you approve it.
Is this just ChatGPT?
Maybe in part. We haven’t decided whether an AI tool, an assistant or both prepare the work. The part we’re testing is the review: whether showing the work makes checking it faster.

What we haven’t decided yet
