Your records go in. A checked sheet comes back.

Right Hand reads the records you already have, matches every line to its source, adds it up, and marks the few things only you can answer. Then it waits for your OK.

  1. Your recordsBank and card exports, the receipts folder, the payroll export. Files you can already download.
  2. Prepared and checkedEach line is matched to its source and the totals are added up and checked.
  3. Questions markedA charge with no receipt or a payment with no memo comes to you as a plain question.
  4. You approveNothing is sent or final until you say so. Your answers are remembered for next month.

The problem, and what we know so far

What we have heard ourselves, what larger surveys say, and the one thing we still have to prove.

Where we started: our problem statement, Oct 15, 2026

We think the main issue is trust in AI and virtual assistants.

What we know, and how we know it

    Our read: the cost of handing work off is the checking afterward. So Right Hand is designed for the check.

    What larger surveys found

      The rule

      Right Hand prepares and you approve. It never moves money, signs for you or files with a government agency, and it sends nothing without your yes. Tax, payroll and legal decisions stay with your CPA, payroll provider or attorney.

      This is a concept list, not a menu of live services. Our next interviews ask which of these you would trust first.

      A second example: a reminder to a client

      Anything a client would read comes to you as a draft first, with the math behind the balance and the late fee left as your call. Tap or hover over a number to see where it came from.

      What 15 minutes with us looks like

      15 minutes by phone, video or in person, at a time you pick. You talk with one of us, check the made-up shop from this page, and tell us what you would still double-check. You get an early look and a say in what we build first. No books to bring, nothing to buy.

      Our hypothesis: owners will hand off routine admin if checking the finished work takes less time than doing it themselves. These walkthroughs are how we test it.

      Before you write to us
      
      
      
        
      Can I use this today?
      Not yet. This page is a clickable prototype, and the walkthroughs decide what we build.
      
        
      Do I have to share my books or bank statements?
      No. The walkthrough uses the made-up shop on this page.
      
        
      Is this a sales call?
      No. There is nothing to buy and no price. We want to hear what you would still check.
      
        
      What if it gets something wrong?
      You see the sources and the math before anything is final. Anything it can’t match to a source comes to you as a question.
      
        
      Is this just ChatGPT?
      We haven’t decided who prepares the work: an AI tool, a trained person or both. Either way, you see where every number came from before you approve it.
      
        
      Two interviews isn’t much. Why believe any of this?
      You shouldn’t yet. Two interviews told us where to look: the checking that comes after work is handed off. The walkthroughs are how we find out whether it holds.
