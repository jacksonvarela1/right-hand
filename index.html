What we heard

Our team started from one problem statement: small business owners are overwhelmed balancing admin tasks with the larger work of running the company. It can lead to lost profit and delayed meetings.

Then came the first customer discovery interviews, with a small business owner and a CEO. Both described the same thing. When they hand work off, they still end up double-checking it or answering follow-up questions about it.

TTeam problem statement, Oct 15, 2026

“Main issue is trust with AI and virtual assistants.”

Interviews so far: a small business owner and a CEO. More are on the way.

So we built Right Hand around the review instead of the handoff. Every finished task comes back with its work shown, and nothing is final until the owner approves it.

What we’re testing next

Our hypothesis: owners will hand off routine admin if checking the finished work takes less time than doing it themselves.

So far we have talked with owners about the problem. Next we put this prototype in front of them and ask:

  • Would this cut the time you spend checking work?
  • What would you still want to verify yourself?
  • Which task would you trust it with first?

Questions we already get

What if it gets something wrong?
You see the sources and the math before anything is final, and anything it isn’t sure about comes to you as a question. Nothing goes out until you approve it.
Is this just ChatGPT?
Maybe in part. We haven’t decided whether an AI tool, an assistant or both prepare the work. The part we’re testing is the review: showing the work so checking it is fast.
